The difference we make

Without us, with us

Without us

  • No known local route. Each owner or dealer looks for a recycler alone.
  • Recycling a pack is a cost: about €738 for NMC or €1,686 for LFP per 400 kg pack, before transport.
  • Unclear paperwork and liability. Is it waste or a used battery? Who is responsible?
  • Packs that still work get recycled too early because nobody tests them.

With us

  • One call. One partner, one written price, paid in advance.
  • Every pack tested. Good ones are reused. The rest are recycled.
  • Papers and test record ready for the battery passport and waste rules.
  • Only intact packs, only with a signed order. We carry the handling risk.

Recycler prices come from the only published 2026 tariff we found (GRS Germany, a ceiling reference, excluding VAT). Latvian prices need real quotes.

Questions investors ask

FAQ

Who pays us?

Importers, dealers and fleets pay a prepaid fee per pack. Who pays today is our first question to answer.

Why not build a recycling plant?

Published models show recycling loses money at today's metal prices and small volumes. We start without one.

Can you promise no losses?

No. We accept a pack only with a signed order and prepayment that covers the quotes plus our fee. Open risks remain, listed above.

Is the volume big enough?

Not in Latvia alone. We plan to source across the Baltics and from abroad, and we will not state a regional number until it is sourced.

What does funding buy first?

Buyer interviews, binding quotes, a permit answer and a first prepaid pack. No plant, no hub lease.

Are these numbers final?

No. Anything marked as an estimate is ours and will change when quotes arrive. Sources are listed below.
