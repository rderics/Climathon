⚖️Without us, with us

🔴 Without us

  • ❓No known local route. Each owner or dealer finds a recycler alone.
  • 💸Recycling a pack is a cost: about €738 (NMC) or €1,686 (LFP) per 400 kg pack, before transport.
  • 📄Unclear paperwork and liability. Waste or “used”? Who is responsible?
  • 🗑️Packs that still work get recycled too early. Nobody tests them.

🟢 With us

  • 📞One call. One partner, one written price, paid in advance.
  • 🔍Every pack tested. Good ones are reused. The rest are recycled.
  • 📑Papers and test record ready for the battery passport and waste rules.
  • 🛡️Only intact packs, with a signed order. We carry the handling risk, not the customer.
Recycler prices: the only published 2026 tariff we found (GRS Germany, ceiling reference, excl. VAT). Latvian prices need real quotes.
