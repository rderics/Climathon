FAQ

Can you say a battery is safe to drive?

No. We report what we found today. The insurer and the car maker decide.

Does a scanner measure battery health?

Cameras only see the casing. Health and cell data come from the car, so we read both.

Who pays us?

The insurer, per check, or a share of the savings. To be tested in a pilot.

Is the market big enough?

Small in Latvia, about 57 checks a year (est.). We start with a pilot and grow across the Baltics.

What if we get it wrong?

One false all clear stops the pilot. We carry professional liability cover first.
