## Main question
How can we give EV batteries a **safe, trusted and profitable** second life — extending life, reducing waste, supporting the circular economy?
Teams may focus on **one link** of the chain: diagnostics/grading · data & battery passport · engineering/re-assembly · a specific application · business/financing · safety/certification.

## Expected outcome (what "strong" looks like)
Concept or prototype + short business case. Show: **the user, value vs new batteries or diesel, the safety approach, next step to a pilot.**
Examples: grading tool (reuse/repurpose/recycle) · second-life product/service for a use case with costs and savings · data/passport/marketplace for trust · safety/certification/liability proposal.

## Facts stated in the brief (cite as "EIT UM brief")
- Packs leave the vehicle at about 70–80 % of original capacity.
- EU Battery Regulation 2023/1542: digital battery passport mandatory from **18 Feb 2027** (EV, light-vehicle, industrial > 2 kWh); a repurposed battery is a **new product with its own passport**.
- Market: Moment Energy 1 GWh repurposing factory (Jun 2026); Rebaba raised $4.6M seed with EIT UM (Sep 2026).
- Latvia: about 1,600 new EVs/yr, EVs about 1.8 % of the fleet, about 5,600 vehicles subsidised until 2025. Riga: up to 100 new trolleybuses + 24 trams planned. The Baltics are building hundreds of MWh of grid batteries after grid synchronisation.
- Problems: SoH alone is not enough (90 % SoH can still fail: self-discharge, imbalance, insulation); too many pack designs; **governance gaps** (classification, liability, safety supervision, testing capacity, insurers fear fire — TalTech 2026); **small volumes** and cheaper new LFP.
- Second life must find use cases where it is clearly cheaper/better: SME solar (about 30 % cheaper than new, France), charger/depot buffers, diesel-generator replacement.

- Latvia supply constraint: about 1,600 new EVs/yr — en.bb.lv/article/business/2026/05/26/in-latvia-the-growth-of-electric-vehicles-has-sharply-slowed-down-the-market-depended-on-subsidies-100290. - Rebaba "CircularHub" in Stockholm scaling to 40 MWh/yr — a Riga hub could copy the model (tech.eu, 9 Sep 2026).
