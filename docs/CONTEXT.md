# Event context

Source: https://climathon.rtu.lv/ (read 2026-10-30)

- **Dates:** 30 Oct – 1 Nov 2026. **Venue:** RTU Science & Innovation Centre (ZIC), 2nd floor "The Moon" hall, Ķīpsalas iela 6A, Riga (remote possible).
- **Themes:** circular economy & green mobility; sustainable urban environments.
- **Our challenge (EIT Urban Mobility Hub):** "Kā piešķirt elektroauto baterijām otru dzīvi?" — ideas that extend battery lifespan, cut resource use, support circular, sustainable mobility.
- **Other challenges (not ours):** Latvijas Zaļais punkts (packaging recycling GHG calculator); Sadales tīkls (reuse of surplus grid cable).

No published scoring rubric. Jury assesses **potential for further development** and **innovativeness**; expects idea → working prototype. Prizes + funding for further development of best ideas.
