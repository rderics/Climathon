## The 5 questions that matter most (ask these first)
1. **Who collects used EV traction batteries in Latvia today, and who pays for it?** (Berga, importers, dealers) — decides whether our hub has a gap or a competitor.
2. **Would anyone pay for an independent battery health test or a trade-in of a swapped pack, and how much?** (Jaunozola, dealers, importers) — decides the front-door revenue.
3. **Do you buy second-life packs, with what spec, volume, price and warranty?** (ENGYcell) — decides the 30%.
4. **What does recycling cost or pay per pack, from Latvia?** (Fortum, AE Elemental, Eneris quotes) — decides the 70%.
5. **What permits and fire rules apply to storing, discharging and shipping used packs in Latvia?** (State Environmental Service, fire service, Mihailovs) — decides whether the hub is legal and insurable.

## Dealers, importers, garages, fleets (phone or visit this week)
Ask 3-5 of: official EV dealers, used-EV importers, independent EV garages, Rīgas satiksme, taxi/delivery fleets, insurers that write off EVs, car dismantlers.
- How many EV packs did you swap or write off last year? What chemistry and make?
- What do you do with the removed pack? Where is it stored, for how long, what does it cost?
- Who takes it away (carmaker, importer, a recycler, abroad)? Do you pay or are you paid, and how much per pack?
- Would you pay for a health test of a used EV before you sell or buy it? How much? Would you give us swapped packs if we took them away free?
- Would you buy a tested used replacement pack? At what price per kWh?
- Which rule or paper blocks you (waste permit, transport, insurer)?

## Authorities and standards (email or call next week)
- **State Environmental Service (VVD):** which waste-management permit do we need for collection, storage, sorting and discharging used EV packs? Does a pack counted as "used" (with a test record) need one? Producer registration for a repurposer?
- **Fire and Rescue Service (VUGD):** requirements for indoor storage of used lithium-ion packs and for a stationary battery unit (spacing, detection, suppression).
- **Latvian register of producers (Elektroregistrs / LETERA, check name):** registration steps and fees for batteries.
- **likumi.lv or Ministry of Environment:** penalty amounts for battery producers who do not comply; any Latvian rule on traction-battery take-back.
- **An insurer:** would you insure a stationary second-life unit, and what do you require?
