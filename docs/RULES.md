# Rules and judging (official regulations, Climathon'26)
Source: Drive `Slides and other documents / CLimathon_Nolikums_Regulations.pdf`.

## Judging — 5 criteria, 20 % each, scored 1–5
1. **Market problem understanding** — clear problem, researched, target customer justified, fit with the real market.
2. **Value proposition** — clear and innovative, matches the problem, end customer and user clearly defined.
3. **Solution** — clear and innovative tech, directly tied to the problem, really delivers the value.
4. **Business model and sustainability** — clear and innovative model; **environmental, social and economic impact assessed**; resource-efficient; viable long-term.
5. **Presentation and Q&A** — story, creativity, answering questions.
Jury focus: **activity and progress during the programme**. At least 50 % of the jury are external experts.

## Pitch format
**3 minutes presentation + 3 minutes Q&A** per team, Sun 1 Nov (12:30–14:00 block). Slides template: Drive `Pitch Submissions / TEAM NAME_CLimathon2026` (Google Slides, currently empty).

## Prizes
Per challenge: **1st = EUR 1,000, 2nd = EUR 500** (total fund EUR 4,500 over 3 challenges), paid to the team leader within 30 days (needs name, personal ID, bank account). Winners must credit RTU Science and Innovation Centre in public communication.

## Other
- Teams: 2–5 people (at least 3 preferred). One person may be in only one team. Max 24 teams. At least one member at every mentor check-in.
- Ideas must be original; the team owns the IP of its idea and technical details. Organisers may publish ideas/solutions with author credit.
