# Used EV batteries for grid balancing in Latvia

30 Oct 2026 · Juris Vitols

## Verdict

Not as a stand-alone balancing business: new LFP storage is cheap, Baltic balancing prices are falling fast, and Latvia retires only about 80 EV packs a year. It can work as a small add-on at a site that needs storage anyway, and a tool that tells owners which case they are in is buildable by Sunday.

- **Prices fell.** A megawatt sold as aFRR in both directions could earn up to €1.47M a year at 2025 average prices, but only €0.14M at October 2026 prices; for mFRR the drop is €0.56M to €0.16M (calculated from TSO price data, capacity payments only).
- **New batteries are cheap.** Stationary battery packs fell 45% in 2025 to $70/kWh, and turnkey systems in Europe averaged $177/kWh ([BNEF](https://about.bnef.com/insights/clean-energy/lithium-ion-battery-pack-prices-fall-to-108-per-kilowatt-hour); [Energy-Storage.news](https://www.energy-storage.news/?p=92133)). Break-even studies say used packs must cost no more than 40–60% of new ones, about $28–42/kWh ([PNNL](https://www.pnnl.gov/publications/techno-economic-assessments-second-life-batteries-electric-vehicle-charging-stations); [Mathews et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2003.03216)).
- **The industry has moved on.** Second life is 0.2% of European grid-scale storage projects, and Finland's Cactos switched to new batteries in 2024 because they had become cheaper ([Benchmark](https://source.benchmarkminerals.com/article/how-is-the-second-life-battery-market-playing-out); [Energy-Storage.news](https://www.energy-storage.news/battery-price-falls-threaten-second-life-model-as-fenecon-opens-assembly-plant-in-germany/)).
- **Local packs are scarce.** Latvia had 13,916 electric cars at the end of 2025, 85% of them registered since 2022, so only about 80 packs a year retire today (estimate) ([Statistics Latvia](https://data.stat.gov.lv/api/v1/en/OSP_PUB/START/NOZ/TR/TRC/TRC012)). Norway removed 4,570 batteries from scrapped cars in 2024 and is the realistic source ([SSB](https://www.ssb.no/natur-og-miljo/avfall/artikler/samferdsel-og-avfall)).
- **Repurposers carry manufacturer duties:** a conformity assessment, new labels and, from 18 February 2027, a new battery passport for each pack ([Arianee](https://www.arianee.com/en/blog/do-recycled-batteries-need-a-battery-passport)).
- **Where it can still work:** sites with weak grid connections such as EV charging hubs and depots, backup that replaces diesel generators or lead-acid, and fleets that own their used packs. Balancing then comes as a bonus through an aggregator. That is idea C4 on the main tab with a revenue layer added.

## Market: what balancing pays and who can bid

Balancing still pays in Latvia, but capacity prices are 70–90% below 2025, and batteries already offer several times the reserve the grid buys.

Finland's battery-saturated market paid about €82k (aFRR) and €37k (mFRR) per megawatt a year at January–June 2026 prices, a sign of where Baltic prices could head ([BBCM Evaluation Report 2026](https://litgrid.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/BBCM-Evaluation-report-2026-1.pdf)).

- **A small market, crowded fast.** In 2026 the three Baltic grids buy 28 MW of FCR, about 100–120 MW of aFRR per direction and about 580–690 MW of mFRR per direction ([Elering](https://www.elering.ee/en/article/baltic-electricity-transmission-operators-announce-plan-phase-out-balancing-resources)). About 14 GW is prequalified, including 1,629 MW of upward aFRR against roughly 120 MW needed ([BBCM Evaluation Report 2026](https://litgrid.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/BBCM-Evaluation-report-2026-1.pdf)).
- **More batteries are coming.** Lithuania had 1,195 MW / 2,261 MWh of batteries in early October 2026 and may pass 2 GW by year-end ([IWR](https://www.renewable-energy-industry.com/news/world/article-7715-battery-storage-boom-in-lithuania-capacity-rises-to-almost-1200-mw-ignitis-and-emsys-vpp-expand-virtual-power-plant-for-balancing-power)). In Latvia, AST's own 80 MW has bid since 30 Oct 2025, NGEN is building 100 MW in Grobiņa and Sunly has 490 MWh contracted from 2027 ([ESS News](https://www.ess-news.com/2025/10/30/latvia-adds-big-batteries-to-complete-grid-sync-with-europe-two-major-estonian-projects-secure-finance/); [LSM](https://eng.lsm.lv/article/economy/economy/10.08.2026-battery-park-to-be-built-in-grobina-latvia.a658185/); [ESS News](https://www.ess-news.com/2026/06/05/rolls-royce-signs-490-mwh-latvia-bess-deal-as-sunly-secures-european-supply/)).
- **Returns shrink.** Aurora gives a new 2-hour battery a 13–16% return if it starts in 2026 and 6–13% if it starts in 2029, when mFRR capacity becomes a small share of revenue ([ESS News](https://www.ess-news.com/2025/10/16/baltic-battery-report-get-in-if-you-can-says-aurora/)). The upside: the TSOs' own zero-price reserves leave the market by November 2028, and mFRR needs grow to 754 MW up and 991 MW down by 2035 ([Elering](https://www.elering.ee/en/article/baltic-electricity-transmission-operators-announce-plan-phase-out-balancing-resources); [AST forecast](https://www.ast.lv/sites/default/files/editor/FRR_dimensioning_forecast_2026-2035_ACEol.pdf)).
- **A small battery needs a pool.** Bids start at 1 MW in 1 MW steps, so a smaller unit must join a group run by a balancing service provider with an AST contract ([BBCM rules](https://www.ast.lv/sites/default/files/editor/Explanatory_document_for_the_Baltic_proposal_to_the_Baltic_balancing_capacity_market_rules_in_accordance_with_EBGL_Articles_33%281%29_and_38%281%29.pdf)). Prequalification takes roughly 2–4 months, and FCR needs inverter power of at least 1.25 times the reserve offered plus 30 minutes at full output ([AST process](https://www.ast.lv/sites/default/files/editor/Pakalpojumi/Regulesana/12_2025_procesu_apraksti/RNV%20tehniskas%20atbilstibas%20izvertesanas%20procesa%20apraksts_30.12.25.pdf); [AST requirements](https://ast.lv/sites/default/files/editor/SVV-info-faili/TOD_S_P_A4_4_01_25.pdf)).
- **Grid charges apply in full.** No storage exemption appears in Sadales tīkls' 2027 tariffs, so a battery on the 6–20 kV grid pays capacity and energy charges like any customer, roughly €20–37k per megawatt a year (estimate from the tariff tables) ([Sadales tīkls](https://sadalestikls.lv/storage/app/media/uploaded-files/Ofici%C4%81lais%20pazi%C5%86ojums%20par%20tarifa%20v%C4%93rt%C4%ABb%C4%81m%202027.pdf)). Sadales tīkls buys no flexibility yet; a pilot with Tet is in development ([CEEnergyNews](https://ceenergynews.com/electricity/latvias-grid-operator-and-tet-to-pilot-new-flexible-electricity-service-for-efficiency/)).
